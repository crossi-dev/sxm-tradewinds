About our property

A classic West Indies building, right over the water.

Built in 1960 and remodeled from top to bottom in 2020, Tradewinds sits directly on the sea in the heart of Simpson Bay. Our best units open onto private balconies with the island's finest sunsets and the constant sound of a soft shorebreak.

Every apartment is fully equipped: dining set, spacious kitchen with its own kitchenware, private bathroom, smart TV and high-speed internet. We provide linens, beach and bath towels and cleaning supplies — you only need to bring sunscreen.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer long-term rentals?

No. The maximum stay is 3 months.

Is the property suitable for babies and children?

There is no lifeguard at the swimming pool, so the property is not ideal for children who need constant supervision.

Is the area safe?

Yes. There is a police station a few meters down the road, and you can walk around at any time, day or night. As anywhere, we encourage guests to take basic precautions, such as keeping valuables in the safe provided in every apartment.

Are pets allowed?

As a rule, we do not accept pets, mainly because of the stray dogs in the area. If you are traveling with a support dog, please let us know in advance. Contact us if you need more information.

Are there extra cleaning fees?

No. All our rates are final. The apartment is cleaned at check-out, and as needed during extended stays.

How do check-in and check-out work?

Check-in is from 15:00; we'll let you know if your apartment is ready earlier. Check-out is by 10:00; please tell us in advance if you need an extension.

We'll meet you on-site to hand over the keys. Please send us your arrival time, along with your flight or boat details in case of delays.

If we can't meet you in person, we'll leave the apartment key in a lockbox and send you the code and instructions.

What are the house rules?
  • Please respect quiet hours: no loud music or disturbances.
  • No parties, and no outside visitors without prior notice.
  • Smoking is allowed in open areas; please dispose of cigarette butts properly.
  • Tuesday evenings can be noisier because of the Latin evening.
  • Please put a towel down when sitting on the outdoor furniture, to keep it clean and comfortable for everyone.
Wi-Fi and TV

We take pride in offering the island's fastest fiber-optic connection. Interruptions are rare, but they can happen. If you need uninterrupted internet for work, we recommend checking your roaming options or getting a prepaid SIM card as a backup.

We don't offer a TV service, but every apartment has a smart TV that connects to your favorite streaming services.

Payments and refunds

We accept cash, wire transfers, Visa, American Express and Mastercard.

Online payments go through Payoneer, and we are working on adding more options.

  • High season and certain other dates: a 25% deposit is required to confirm the reservation. It is fully refundable if you cancel up to 5 days before arrival (bank fees and credit card commissions cannot be refunded).
  • Low season: no deposit is required, except for multiple-room reservations, which follow the high-season rules.

If you book through a third-party platform, that platform's policy applies.

Is there parking?

We don't have private parking, but you can park on the street in front of the building. The area is safe.

What can I find in the area?

We are in the heart of Simpson Bay, right next to the Simpson Bay Bridge, one of the busiest areas of Sint Maarten. Dozens of bars, pubs, restaurants, banks, grocery stores, souvenir shops and casinos are within walking distance.

Across the bridge, Kimsha Beach has beach bars, boat tours, water sports rentals, diving schools and more. Simpson Bay Beach is just a few meters from our entrance; it sits in a more residential area, so it is quieter and has fewer bars.
